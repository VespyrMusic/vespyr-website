About

Music for the hour when the city goes violet.

Vespyr makes hypnotic melodic trap and boom bap for the hours after sunset. Slow-burning melodies, heavy low end and drums that take their time.

The name comes from the Latin vesper: evening, and the evening star that shows up first when the sky turns dark. Every track is built for that moment. Night drives, late study sessions, the gym after hours, or just the city lights through a window.

The lion on the throne is the Vespyr king. He doesn't chase anything. He waits, and the night comes to him.
